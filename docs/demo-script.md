# Demo script

Use this path for a short recording. Sign in as `hr@acme.test` / `acme-hr-demo` unless the password was changed before seeding.

1. Open the app and sign in. The home screen is titled Pay questions.
2. Point out the active headcount and the annual payroll in USD. Read the line that the USD figure uses the 15 January 2026 snapshot and is not an amount to pay.
3. Show payroll in local currencies, then the same countries compared in USD.
4. Open the rate snapshot so the conversion is visible.
5. Choose a country and show who is paid the most and the least in that country’s currency.
6. Open People. Search for an employee number, or follow a country from the home screen. The list is paged.
7. Open one person. Change the annual salary and save.
8. Show the revision: previous amount, new amount, and time.
9. Return to Pay questions and show that the USD comparison total moved by that change.
10. From People, use Export CSV to download the current sheet.

Payroll, tax, approvals, and employee self-service are intentionally absent. The requirements note explains why.
